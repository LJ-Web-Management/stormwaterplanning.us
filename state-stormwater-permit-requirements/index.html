State Stormwater Permit Requirements

Nearly every U.S. state runs its own construction stormwater permitting program, each with its own permit language and its own training or inspector expectations. Here's a reference summary of permitting authorities and links, organized by state.

Why Requirements Vary by State

Roughly 47 states are authorized by EPA to run their own NPDES construction stormwater permitting programs in place of the federal Construction General Permit. Each state agency writes its own permit, which can include its own terminology, its own inspector or "qualified person" standard, and in some cases a state-specific credential. The table below summarizes several commonly asked-about states. See Stormwater Regulations & Standards for the federal framework these state programs build on.
