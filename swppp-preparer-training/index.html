## WHAT THIS COURSE IS
Document-focused online training for the person who writes and revises the SWPPP itself:
interpreting Construction General Permit (CGP) requirements, assessing site hazards and
risk, selecting BMPs by design criteria, and documenting updates through final
stabilization. It is not the course for field inspections only (see
/qualified-swppp-practitioner-qsp-training/) and it is not California QSD state
qualification (see /federal-qualified-person-vs-california-qsp-qsd/).

## DISCLAIMERS
- Permit operators remain responsible for ensuring that stormwater team members meet the
  training requirements of the applicable federal, state, or local permit.
- Anyone can take this federal SWPPP training course. However, some states have their own
  SWPPP or QSP/QSD training courses with additional certification requirements, such as in
  California, New York, and Washington. In these states, a state-approved course may be
  required, this applies even if you have already completed a federal CGP-based course.

  completion, not California QSD state qualification. Some states use different titles for
  SWPPP preparers, designers, developers, inspectors, or qualified persons; always verify
  your project's permit and state requirements.
- This site does not claim EPA approval/endorsement, NPDES certification, or California
  QSP/QSD certification for this course.

Read Before You Enroll

Credential Transparency & State-Specific Notes

stormwaterplanning.us does not claim EPA approval, EPA endorsement, NPDES certification, or California QSP/QSD certification for this course. A certificate of completion documents that training content was completed, it is not a government-issued credential.

Permit Operator Responsibility

Permit operators remain responsible for ensuring that stormwater team members meet the training requirements of the applicable federal, state, or local permit.

State-Specific Requirements

Anyone can take this federal SWPPP training course. However, some states have their own SWPPP or QSP/QSD training courses with additional certification requirements, such as in California, New York, and Washington. In these states, a state-approved course may be required, this applies even if you have already completed a federal CGP-based course.

QSP vs. QSD

A QSD (Qualified SWPPP Developer) is responsible for developing and revising the SWPPP; a QSP (Qualified SWPPP Practitioner) is responsible for implementing and inspecting the plan in the field. This course covers core SWPPP preparation knowledge, particularly the development and documentation work associated with the QSD role, but is a certificate of completion, not California QSD state qualification. See Federal vs. California QSP/QSD for details.
